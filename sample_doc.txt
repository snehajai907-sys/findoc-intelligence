ANNUAL FINANCIAL SUMMARY 2023

Total Revenue: $4.2 billion, representing a 12% increase 
year-over-year driven by strong performance in the Technology 
and Financial Services segments.

Operating Expenses: $2.8 billion including $450 million in 
Research and Development investments, up 18% from prior year.

Net Income: $1.1 billion with a net margin of 26.2%.

Debt Position: The company holds $800 million in long-term 
debt against $1.4 billion in cash and equivalents, resulting 
in a net cash position of $600 million.

Outlook: Management projects revenue growth of 8-10% for 
fiscal year 2024, contingent on macroeconomic stability.
